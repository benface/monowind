# Top layer implementation plan

Status: **implemented** (2026-09-13; every phase green, the two
goldens recorded). Spec: `top-layer.md` — normative; `positioning.md`
for the `fixed` box it shares a mechanism with; this plan only
sequences them. Phase 1 of `2026-09-13-components.md`. What shipped
differently from the phases below:

- The light elements: a top-layer element resolves against the
  viewport whatever its `position` (probed: the host at 150,100 and
  its popover at 0,0), so `render.ts` flags it `data-mw-top` and the
  companion places it `position: fixed` from the grid's client origin,
  which the host writes as `--mw-ox`/`--mw-oy` after each layout and
  page scroll; a fixed box off the stack keeps its parent-relative
  placement and takes its ancestors' scroll (and sticky shifts) back
  through `--mw-sx`/`--mw-sy`, collected with the sticky boxes.
- The walk carries the current grid's unclipped `put` (`Walk.put`),
  which a fixed box paints through past its scroller's clipping put;
  the positioning pass writes `hostRect` on every fixed box.
- A stack element paints with its own opacity alone (the top layer
  escapes its ancestors'), the story catching the omission.
- The backdrop is a box the browser draws, not a tint of the cells:
  the first cut composited every cell's colors, which cost a pass per
  paint, doubled where an overhanging font's spans overlap (the
  teletype theme), and could show no blur or gradient; a backdrop's
  element is a layer root instead, its backdrop box placed beneath its
  own by the layer pass.

## Shape

Three things are new: the read and the stack (which elements are in
the top layer, in what order), a paint and hit path from the host's
origin that `fixed` boxes take too, and the backdrop tint. The
layout needs only the reader's UA defaults; the layers, the covers,
and the color compositing are reused as they are.

## Phases (each ends green: `pnpm check` + the visual sweep)

### 0. Probe

A temporary `zz-probe` story, deleted after: a native `::backdrop`
under the companion's lock is invisible on screen while
`getComputedStyle(el, "::backdrop")` still reads the authored color
under `[measuring]`; a `display` transition on a `[popover]` survives
a measuring pass with the scoped mask, in Chromium; a grid container
with `transition-discrete` starts no transition under the degrid
assist in Firefox.

### 1. The read, the defaults, and the stack

- style.ts: `CellStyle.topLayer` from `el.matches(":popover-open,
:modal")`; `CellStyle.backdrop` from the `::backdrop`'s
  `background-color`, null when transparent; for a top-layer element,
  insets that read `auto` become 0, sizes that read `auto` become
  `fit-content`, margins are `auto` unless a class or the inline style
  sets them (the layout's centering already follows from these,
  probed).
- `top-layer.ts`: the stack — a `Map<Element, number>` of entry
  order fed by the host's capture-phase `toggle` listener on `open`,
  elements found open without a record appended in tree order at the
  layout; a query for the order of a node's element.
- element.ts: the listener added and removed with the others, either
  toggle state scheduling a layout; `display` and `overlay` join the
  landing transitions in `#onTransitionDone`, for elements with
  `topLayer`.
- styles.css: `display, overlay` appended to the transition mask for
  `mono-wind[measuring] :is([popover], dialog)` and the `[settling]`
  twin; `[popover]::backdrop, dialog::backdrop { background:
transparent !important; backdrop-filter: none !important }` outside
  `[measuring]`.
- Tests: the read (a popover open and closed, a modal and a
  non-modal dialog, the defaults with and without authored classes,
  the backdrop color); the stack's order and tree-order fallback.

### 2. Boxes from the host's origin

- plain-text.ts `walk`: a `fixed` child recurses with the host's
  origin in the current grid and no ancestor clip, unclipped by its
  scroller and unscrolled; a `topLayer` child is skipped in place and
  collected; after the tree, the stack's elements walk from the root
  in stack order, each with a host-relative origin (the ancestors'
  unscrolled origins summed), on the main grid.
- pointer.ts `hitStack`: the stack tested first, top down, from the
  root; a `fixed` child hit from the host's origin, its ancestors'
  clips ignored.
- Layers: a layer root inside a top-layer element opens its box after
  every other, which `paintLayers` already orders by the walk.
- Tests: a fixed box inside a scroller painted and hit at the host's
  cells, unmoved by the scroll (the probe of 2026-09-13 as a test);
  the stack after a later sibling, outside a scroller, two elements in
  order, a layer under and inside; the pointer through the stack.

### 3. The backdrop tint

- plain-text.ts: before a stack element with a backdrop paints, every
  cell of the store and of every closed layer takes the tint —
  `backgroundColor` composited over the cell's or the host's,
  `color` over the glyph's or the host's (`compositeColors`, with the
  host's colors read as the grid's base).
- Tests: cells with and without backgrounds, glyph colors, a layer's
  cells, opaque and translucent tints, two stacked backdrops.

### 4. Stories, goldens, docs

- A `Top layer` story file: a popover by `popovertarget` from inside a
  scrolling list; a modal dialog with `backdrop:bg-black/50`, the
  page inert; a nested popover; an `@starting-style` enter and, in
  Chromium, a discrete `display` exit; a text-mode drag over a
  popover. Goldens of the dialog and the popover.
- positioning.md's `fixed` bullet as shipped; `cell-model.md` pointers;
  `core-architecture.md` on the top layer; the spec's status.
