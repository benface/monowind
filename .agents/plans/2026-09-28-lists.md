# List markers

Status: **done 2026-09-28**, one commit (the user's call: lists land
whole, stylable, with their stories). The spec is
`.agents/specs/lists.md` (normative: rules, probes, deviations,
tests); this plan ordered the work onto the code as it stood after
95c3a94. Its deviation numbers are the spec's as it stood: generated
content removed 6 and 8 and renumbered the rest (7 → 6, 9 → 7).

## Scope

In: everything the spec states — markers for every
`display: list-item` element (`li`, `summary`, the `list-item` utility), the
`list-*` utilities and the `marker:` variant, CSS Lists 3 numbering
with HTML's `start`, `reversed` and `value`, `counter()` and
`counters()` in a marker's `content`, the simple predefined counter
styles and same-origin `@counter-style` rules, inside and outside
placement, the marker's paint, hit and copy, and the light DOM's
locks.

Out:

- **`::before` and `::after` content**, the next feature after this
  one (its own plan, in 0.3.4). The grid draws no generated content
  today, so a `before:content-[counter(step)]` draws nothing, counter
  or not; the spec's deviation 6 (a pseudo-element's counter
  properties count nothing) follows from it. Generated text is its own
  feature: runs, selection, copy and a native lock. The counter walk
  here takes records in tree order, so a pseudo-element's record slots
  in before and after its element's children when that lands.
- The complex predefined counter styles (spec deviation 2).

## What it builds on

The deviation batch left most of the machinery in place:

- **The outside marker's row** is the item's first baseline, which
  layout.ts `baselineRow(node)` already finds: a leaf's first line, or
  the first in-flow child's with one, past floats, out-of-flow boxes
  and boxes with no line.
- **The inside marker's cells** are the first line's, before the text:
  the leaf's resolved `node.indent` already feeds the wrap's
  `firstLineIndent` (`wrapOptions`) and `lineStart`'s `indent`, and the
  intrinsic widths count it. Balance, pretty, justify, truncation and
  line-clamp all go through those two.
- **The native first line** takes the engine's indent through
  render.ts `nativeIndent` (`--mw-ti`), a leaf's or its first
  anonymous run's.
- **Floats**: a leaf's first line band (`lineBands[0].x`) is the
  offset the outside marker's end moves in by.
- **Paint order and hit**: stacking.ts `contentOf` visits a node's
  text in its content turn; `paintIndex` records each paint for
  pointer.ts `lastTaking`.
- **Glyph roles**: the solid tables carry the scrollbar's
  (`scrollTrack`, `scrollThumb`, glyphs.ts `scrollGlyphs`); the marker
  roles join them.
- **`marker:text-lg`**: the inner font-size warning's class scan
  already matches it.
- **`sr-only`**: style.ts reads a zero-clipped element's display as
  `none` (`isZeroClipped`), so the tree drops it, while the spec
  counts it; the counter walk reads `display` itself.

## Milestones

Each step's tests are seen red first, against the working tree before
the change or a scratch copy.

### 0. Probes (done 2026-09-28)

Recorded in the spec ("Inside", "`details` and `summary`"):

- Chromium and Firefox justify through an inside marker's spaces,
  WebKit not; the engine follows the first two (CSS: the marker is
  the line's inline content). WebKit's range rects leave justification
  out, so its justified lines were read by ink.
- `balance` and `pretty` count the marker as an equal `text-indent`,
  in all three: the indent path serves unchanged.
- Truncation keeps the marker; a box narrower than it cuts it
  (Chromium, Firefox; WebKit keeps it whole).
- `line-clamp-*` on an item removes its marker in all three (its
  `-webkit-box` is no `list-item`), which the display read gives for
  free.
- `summary` computes alike in all three, and `::marker` styles its
  triangle in WebKit too: that risk is gone.
- **Found**: a closed `details` draws its hidden content on the grid,
  in all three engines, before and after a toggle — step 1, with
  `hidden="until-found"` and `content-visibility: hidden`, found
  alike in the review.

### 1. Skipped contents (tree.ts)

A `details` without `open` builds its first `summary` alone, and a
box whose `content-visibility` is `hidden` (`hidden="until-found"`
too, found in the review) builds empty: their contents are no nodes,
and the counter walk skips them as Chromium and Firefox do (WebKit
counts a closed `details`'s items). The light DOM's attribute
observer already relays out on a toggle. A fix of today's grid,
independent of markers (visibility.md "Skipped contents").

Tests: tree.test.ts, and the `Details` and `SkippedContents` stories,
red first against the last commit.

### 2. Counter styles (counters.ts)

css-counter-styles-3's algorithm over its simple predefined styles as
data: the six systems, `prefix`, `suffix`, `range`, `pad`, `negative`
and `fallback`, a representation past 60 code points falling back.
`@counter-style` rules are read from the CSSOM lazily, on the first
name a rule may define (any but `decimal`, `disc`, `circle`, `square`
and the disclosure triangles), from the host's root's stylesheets and
its document's, adopted and imported ones included, under the `@layer`,
`@media` and `@supports` rules whose conditions hold, and cached per
sheet until its top-level rules change (spec deviation 9). A name
still unknown draws as `decimal`. The bullets resolve against the
item's glyph set in step 8.

Tests (lists.test.ts): each style at 1, its range's ends, 0 and a
negative; `decimal-leading-zero`'s pad under a sign; the fallbacks;
an `@counter-style` rule of each system; an unknown name.

### 3. Numbering (counters.ts)

The counter walk as a pure function over records in tree order — an
element's `counter-reset`, `counter-set` and `counter-increment`, its
list-item flag, and HTML's `start`, `reversed` and `value` — with a
DOM adapter (tree.ts `counterTree`) that builds the records from the
host's light DOM,
skipping `display: none` subtrees and skipped contents (a closed
`details`'s, a `content-visibility: hidden` box's), and counting
`sr-only` ones. Scopes, CSS's order per element, `list-item`'s
implicit increment, reversed counting back from the scope's end,
author CSS over the hints, and every counter a marker's `content`
names.

The walk runs only where a marker reads a value — none for strings and
bullets — so a host without numbered lists reads no counter property.
Its reads come after the tree's, when every style is already resolved.

Tests: every case under the spec's "Numbering" as a table, the
engines' value where they agree and CSS's (Firefox's) where they
split; `display: none` skipped, `sr-only` counted, a custom counter.

### 4. Reads (style.ts, types.ts)

`CellStyle.marker` (`MarkerStyle`): a list item's parts — its
`::marker`'s `content`, else its `list-style-type` — its position
(inside for an inline item whatever it says), its color (none where
it is the item's, so a transition's frames reach it), its glyph values
and whether its `content` holds an image, read for a list item alone:
one `getComputedStyle(el, "::marker")` each. `display` holding
`list-item` marks the item.

Tests (lists.test.ts): each read, `content`'s parts, `list-none` under
a string `content`.

### 5. Markers on nodes (tree.ts, layout.ts)

- tree.ts: after the build, the walk's values and the reads make each
  item's `LayoutNode.marker` — its text, advances and position. An
  inside marker before a leading block is an anonymous run of the
  marker alone; an empty inside item is one row, the marker's.
- layout.ts, inside: the marker's cells join the leaf's first-line
  indent after `text-indent` (the wrap's `firstLineIndent`,
  `lineStart`), so wrapping, balance, pretty and alignment treat them
  as the probes found, and the intrinsic widths count them. The
  marker's x is its line's start less its cells. On a justified first
  line the marker's spaces join the line's gaps (`justifySpread`
  counting them, `spreadBefore` placing the text after them).
- layout.ts, outside: the marker ends at the item's border-box start,
  moved in by the first line's float band for a leaf, on
  `baselineRow(item)`, or on the first content row where the item has
  no line. An item with no in-flow content is one row unless a height
  says otherwise; one holding only an empty block is none.

Tests: the spec's inside and outside placement lists
(lists.test.ts, on node trees).

### 6. Paint, hit and copy (stacking.ts, plain-text.ts, pointer.ts)

- `contentOf` calls a visitor `marker` before the node's text, so the
  marker paints at the start of its item's content turn: over in-flow
  fills and floats, under positioned boxes. `paintIndex` records it,
  and a marker cell takes the hit for its item.
- plain-text.ts paints the cells in the `::marker`'s color, weight and
  style, under the item's clips, visibility and group opacity; an
  inside marker takes its item's propagated decorations, an outside
  one none; glyph roles through the item's set. A truncated first line
  cuts an inside marker too where the box is narrower than it.
- A marker cell is never highlighted (it is no character of its
  leaf); the copy puts a shown marker before its item's first text
  where the selection holds that line whole (selection.ts
  `copyMarker`, the user's call over the browsers'); grid mode's drag
  copies what it shows.

Tests (lists.test.ts): paint cases and `renderPlainText` rows, the
hit, the copy.

### 7. The light DOM (render.ts, styles.css)

- Locks, gated on the read flag: `list-style-position: outside` and
  `list-style-image: none` on every light element, and
  `[data-mw-marker-image]::marker { content: none }`; ungated, every
  `::marker` at zero `font-size` and `line-height`, which takes its
  ink, hit and line height (the planned inherited font left an outside
  marker's hit hanging past its item).
- render.ts: an inside marker's cells, and on a justified line their
  spaces' share of its spread, join `--mw-ti` on the element whose
  first line it opens (`nativeIndent`), so the native line, justified
  over its text's gaps alone, lands on the grid's words;
  `data-mw-marker-image` on an item whose marker `content` holds an
  image.

Tests: lists.test.ts, cascade.test.ts (the locks in the lock layer).

### 8. Glyph roles (glyphs.ts)

The solid tables gain `disc`, `circle`, `square`, `disclosureOpen` and
`disclosureClosed`, defaulting to `• ◦ ▪ ▼ ▶` (glyphs.ts
`BULLET_GLYPHS`); `ascii` maps them to `* o # v >`, `cp437` to
`• ○ ■ ▼ ►`; `--mw-missing-glyphs` honored per glyph; counters.ts
`withBullets` draws a bullet, and a rule extending one, through the
item's set. Tests: lists.test.ts.

### 9. Stories, visual and bench

- `scripts/bench.mjs` gains a `lists` shape: time to interactive

## Risks

- **`::marker` reads.** A pseudo-element's computed style resolves on
  read. Measured: 1,000 preflight `list-none` items load level with
  the last commit, so every item reads.
- **The CSSOM scan** of a large stylesheet (Tailwind's) happens only
  for a redefinable name, once per sheet until its top-level rules
  change; a rule inserted deeper waits (spec deviation 9).
- **Stylesheet changes** trigger no relayout today, so a new
  `@counter-style` rule shows at the next layout.
